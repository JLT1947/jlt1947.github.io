This is the KLOCHENDLER portion of my family tree. It was initially compiled on paper in 1961 with my father Jacques and grandmother Léa Tancerman, and grand-uncle David Klochendler, and I've erratically maintained it through the past 45 years or so, with the help of cousins over 2 continents. It is however quite incomplete, as the family has branched out exponentially through 4 or even 5 generations since then, and I would deeply appreciate all relevant input regarding family members past and present: names, dates of birth, etc. Hope you enjoy this genealogy  in its present state.
