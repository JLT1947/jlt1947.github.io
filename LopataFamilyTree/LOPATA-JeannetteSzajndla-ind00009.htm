Jeannette (Szajndla) LOPATA

Jeannette (Szajndla) was born in 1921.  Jeannette (Szajndla)'s father was Albert (Abraham-Izhak) LOPATA and her mother was Régine ROZENBAUM.  Her paternal grandparents were Israel LOPATA and Esther Hudla ROZGA (Rosgow); her maternal grandparents were Samuel ROZENBAUM and Szajndla FUKS. She had a sister named Roony (Rachel).  She was the older of the two daughters.  She died at the age of 97 on June 10th, 2018.
